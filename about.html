★ About Us =(^w^= ★

Hi! We're the Digital Star System, something resembling a collective of anime girls in a trenchcoat.

We're a graphic designer! Our work can be seen in projects such as Constant Companions (Deluxe Edition), Blades of Ether and Sonic Runners Revival. If you're a vocal-synth fan, you may also recognize our work on this set of computer stickers! Over the years, our work has been seen on LegalEagle, SnapCube, and more!

In another life, we were a content creator by the name KODA...

We've always had an appreciation for the lightweight and simple. But as time marches on, the ever-increasing bloated landscape of the modern internet continues to abandon the machines of the past - and eroding with it the art of a truly personal and moderated space.

We strive to keep The Jukebox optimized and clean, without sacrificing style. With a minimal reliance on anything other than HTML and CSS, we aim to keep it a pleasant stop for all users, regardless of tech spec (including mobile optimizations!).

And with the capitalistic hellscape of Web 2.0 slowly eating away at what little charm the internet truly has left, we advocate for truly decentralized solutions to help keep a flourishing network of beautiful art and inspiring self-expression alive.

Thanks for taking the time to visit. If you want to get ahold of us, either regarding this site or just to say hello, you can reach us via the social icons on the right, or feel free to leave feedback and comments in the guestbook! Thank you!
