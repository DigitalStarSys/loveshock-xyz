The Story

Sekai is a rather unremarkable machine; it consists primarily of hand-me-down parts from our own and others' builds. Our needs eventually grew past Starfall's <4TB of usable space, so Starfall was given gaming duties, and Origin Point's old motherboard and CPU were repurposed.
